# Champion Swords

## Description

Champion Swords is a vanilla-friendly open-source mod that adds 16 new swords with custom abilities to Minecraft!

## Features

Champion Swords adds three new tiers of swords. 
Every sword can be made by merging the sword's handle, a Diamond, and a Diamond Sword in a Smithing Template. 
A sword can be turned back into its handle by a crafting table.

### Tier One Swords
The handles of tier one swords can be obtained in many ways. These do a base of 8 damage.

- **Sword of Echoes** - Obtained from killing the Warden, shoots sonically charged blasts
- **Sword of Undying** - Obtained from a Master Weaponsmith, gives regenerative effects
- **Sword of Trials** - Obtained from Ominous Vaults, allows you to dash forwards
- **Sword of the Elder** - Obtained from killing Elder Guardians, inflicts Mining Fatigue 3 and Slowness 3
- **Sword of Ashes** - Obtained from killing the Wither, shoots Wither Skulls
- **Sword of Blazing** - Obtained from killing Blazes, grants Fire Resistance and sets enemies on fire
- **Sword of the Brute** - Obtained from killing Piglin Brutes, grants Strength 3
- **Sword of Souls** - Obtained from killing Ghasts, grants Health Boost 2
- **Sword of the Eye** - Obtained from killing Endermen, shoots Ender Pearls
- **Sword of Crystals** - Obtained from crafting, explodes nearby enemies
- **Sword of the Shulker** - Obtained from killing Shulkers, gives Resistance 5 and Slowness 5
- **Sword of the Dragon** - Obtained from killing the Ender Dragon, shoots Dragon Fireballs

### Tier Two Swords
Tier two handles are obtained by merging tier one handles of their respective dimension. These do a base of 12 damage.

- **Sword of the Hero(Overworld)** - Grants Strength 2, Speed 2, and Hero of the Village 5
- **Sword of the Inferno(Nether)** - Shoots a flurry of Fireballs and Wither Skulls 
- **Sword of the Void(End)** - Pushes away and damages enemies near you drastically

### The Champion Sword
The handle for the Champion Sword is obtained by merging all tier two handles. This does 24 damage.

- **Sword of the Champion** - Grants various positive effects to the wielder and various negative effects to damaged enemies
